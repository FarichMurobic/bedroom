# Bedroom

Bedroom is a platform for sharing imagination, creativity, innovation, and skills through writing.

The platform provides a space where people can turn their ideas, thoughts, experiences, knowledge, and skills into written content and share them with others. It is designed to encourage people to express what they imagine, create, learn, and discover through meaningful and creative writing.

Through written content, users can share their perspectives, knowledge, experiences, creative works, and innovative ideas, while discovering different ways of thinking, creating, and developing skills.

## Project Status

> Bedroom is currently under active development.

The project is being developed incrementally, with the core architecture and foundational systems being established before the platform's main writing and content-sharing features are introduced.
